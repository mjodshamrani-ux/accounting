This separate pre-code addendum extends the reviewed draft without changing its 72 sealed payloads, frozen economic rows, nine positive CSVs, original failures or existing section families. The original draft manifest is `83c0ce2ccafa08bfff3f20bfa0831eae145434c98ba779cada4c421d38afda79`; its frozen-input manifest is `1b9612a623e3055fe6fa369eb4b7b175da67ab04e07f0895e42b112fb251c7c4`. This addendum is not implementation acceptance.

The original KWD literals `-12.500`, `+2.005`, `0.000` admit two financially different numeric interpretations under exponent3. Dot-decimal gives minor units `[-12500, 2005, 0]`; interpreting dot as grouping gives `[-12500000, 2005000, 0]`. The qualified header proves KWD, and the precision policy proves exponent3, but neither determines which numeric interpretation the accountant intends. Setting `numberFormat: dot` without an explicit bound original choice is insufficient. The first preserved native proof correctly refused ambiguous preparation; this is not a reason to change expected money.

## Original review obligation

For the three frozen KWD positive originals, explicit section review must include the separate acknowledgement `originalDotInterpretationReviewed: true` and a number-format choice for the original PDF with value `dot`. The reviewer sees the original amount cells and the two interpretation values, confirms dot decimal as the intended original interpretation, and supplies the ordinary reviewer label/rationale. Neither the qualifier, exponent lookup, model, default setting, inferred patch nor derived CSV may manufacture that answer.

The bound original choice must match a fresh native original replay and current reading: original SHA256, extraction SHA256/revision, selected sheet and header, amount column, currency and exponent, exact current allowed number-format candidate set, PDF cuts, excluded rows and balance inputs. `formatChoice` uses zero-based sheet/header/columns; literal cell evidence uses one-based physical row/page/column. This finite family admits only sheet0, header0, amount column3, signed mode, multiplier+1, no exclusions or balances, and the original declared cuts. All three frozen KWD choices have the candidate set `{dot, comma}`; the addendum records it canonically as `[dot, comma]`. Reject missing, extra, duplicate or changed candidates even if the stored selected value remains dot. Fresh candidate reconstruction and agreement with the original source/read context are required; a supplied candidate array is not its own proof.

Receipt ownership remains live-session ownership. The interpretation acknowledgement and the complete original choice enter the guarded review/selection context and reviewer hash. Any change to the original file, extraction, reading, code/exponent, acknowledgement, candidate set or choice fields invalidates the pending selection and owned receipt before and after an await. Ambiguous originals without this review cannot mint a currency derivation receipt or publish a partial artifact. SAR and JPY cases whose allowed interpretations have identical values remain eligible under the prior draft without a new redundant ambiguity acknowledgement; their current dot setting and exponent still belong to the bound reading context.

The new choice is a carried interpretation from the live original review, not a second financial decision or autonomous numeric inference. Prepare the fresh derived CSV under KWD3 with this derived choice and confirmed financial/scope flags false.
