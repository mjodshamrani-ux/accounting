# Provenance and adaptation

This is an original, project-specific synthesis of design methods, not a concatenation of upstream skills. It adapts product character, visual-world thinking and deliberate refinement from **Unslop**, and practical alignment, typography, density and component-state inspection from **Unslop AI UI**. It replaces generic palettes, bans and recipes with Tarasuf identity, financial evidence, twelve-domain UX, accessibility and verification. No upstream images, fonts, executable scripts or substantial code examples are included.
